01 / THE ENGINE

Built for contact.
Built for motion.

Use the pieces you need, or step an entire physical body on the server.

01

Compound collision

Oriented boxes follow real model parts. Swept checks catch fast contacts with thin geometry.

02

Automatic aerodynamics

Thin collision volumes can become lifting surfaces with drag, torque, and fall behavior.

03

Rigid-body motion

One server-side step integrates velocity, rotation, world contact, and impact response.

04

Hinges & folding

Drive moving parts with torque, limits, resistance, and contact-driven pressure.

05

Moving worlds

Optional external collision providers connect moving structures, including Sable ships through Create: The Air War.

06

Debuggable geometry

See every assembled collision part as a green outline with F3+B.

02 / HANDS ON

Try physics
inside the game.

No adapter needed to experiment. The creative tab includes three tools for building, moving, and removing physical objects.

All tools and commands ↗
01

Block Assembler

Select two opposite corners to make one body from real block shapes. Supports up to 128 blocks.

▧
02

Physics Wand

Hold right click to grab. Scroll to change distance. Hold Tab and move the mouse to rotate.

✣
03

Entity Deleter

Point at a physical body and remove it with one click.
