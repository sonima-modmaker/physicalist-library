MORE THAN A PLAYGROUND

WHAT IS PHYSICALIST?

A standalone NeoForge mod and API for physical things in Minecraft. Build with it directly, or use the engine inside another mod.

▧

BLOCKS WITH WEIGHT

Assemble real blocks into one body with their voxel shapes. Push it, pick it up, and watch it settle.

➤

SHAPES THAT FLY

Thin model parts can act as wings. Motion and damage change the way a vehicle handles.

⌘

READY FOR MODS

Compound collision, hinges, and server-side motion are available through a Java API.

Create: The Air War connects its rockets and Sable ships to Physicalist. Immersive Vehicles Refurbished is a planned integration, not a current dependency.

READY TO BUILD?

GET PHYSICALIST

For Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.219–21.1.251. Download the playable JAR, put one copy in your instance's mods folder, then launch the game.
