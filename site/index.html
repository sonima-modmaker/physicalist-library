What's inside

One physics engine, many things it can move

Physicalist is a standalone NeoForge mod and a Java API. Play with its tools in creative, or build its bodies into your own mod.

Blocks with weight

Assemble real blocks into one body with their exact voxel shapes. Push it, lift it, tip it over and watch it settle.

Chains and ragdolls

Articulated chains in a shared native scene, and players that go limp and tumble as ragdolls.

Water that floats things

Water buoyancy for physical bodies: what you drop in a lake floats, bobs and drifts.

Smoke, heat and clouds

A native fluid solver: hot smoke rises, fills rooms, pours out of doors, and a big fire's plume climbs into clouds.

Soft bodies Experimental

Vehicles made of nodes and beams that dent and crumple, hinged doors that latch, and a flyable plane with flexing wings.

Ready for other mods

Compound collision, automatic wing lift from thin boxes, hinges and a soft-body API with road wheels.

Create: The Air War links its rockets and Sable ships to Physicalist.

For mod developers

Put a soft body in the world in a few calls

Build a structure from any model (GLB, glTF, OBJ, FBX or Blockbench JSON), give it road wheels, and let Physicalist step it on its own thread. Read back node positions, wheel states and a rigid frame every tick.

Get started

Install in a minute

  1. Download the 0.16.4 JAR from GitHub releases.
  2. Drop it in your instance's mods folder — one copy only.
  3. Launch Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge and open the creative tab.
